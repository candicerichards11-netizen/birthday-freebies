💡 Tips & Guides

Get the most out of every birthday freebie!

📅 4 Weeks Before Your Birthday

1Sign up for all restaurant loyalty apps — Spur, Mugg & Bean, Nando's, Steers, Krispy Kreme, Kauai & RocoMamas. Most require prior membership.

2Register your birthday on Dis-Chem, Clicks, The Body Shop, Sorbet & Truworths — vouchers arrive via email and you'll miss them if you sign up late.

3Book the Stellenbosch Wine Tractor and Durbanville Wine Safari — both require advance booking and are very popular.

4Sign up for the Cape Wheel Birthday Club at capewheel.co.za/clubs — your voucher is emailed on your birthday so register before.

5Join Ster-Kinekor SK Club and buy at least 1 ticket first to activate your birthday freebie.

🎂 Ideal Birthday Day Plan

Table Mountain → Two Oceans Aquarium → Cape Wheel → Waterfront Duck → Dinner at RocoMamas. That's 5 freebies in one day — all free with your SA ID! Use the "Plan My Day" button on the freebies page for a personalised itinerary.

Most restaurant freebies are valid 7–30 days around your birthday — spread them across the month rather than rushing everything into one day.

❓ FAQ

Everything you need to know.
What if they refuse my freebie?
Politely ask for a manager and show your SA ID and loyalty app. If still refused, contact the brand's customer service. Always call ahead — offers can change without notice.
Do I always need my SA ID?
Yes — almost always. A green ID book, smart ID card, driver's licence or passport. Always carry it!
Can I claim freebies on a different day?
Many yes — most restaurants are valid 7 days before/after. Table Mountain and Two Oceans are valid the whole birthday month. Robben Island, Total Ninja and UpCycles require your actual birthday only.
Are wine farm freebies really free?
Usually a complimentary glass or tasting — not a free full meal. Always call ahead to confirm what is included.
When should I sign up for loyalty apps?
At least 4 weeks before your birthday. Krispy Kreme requires 48hrs minimum; Sorbet and SK Club require prior activity first.
Can kids claim freebies?
Yes! Use the 👶 Kids filter. Always bring a birth certificate or ID as proof of birthday and age.
How do I save this as a phone app?
iPhone/iPad: Open in Safari → Share ⬆️ → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen.
How do I export my personalised checklist?
Tap the 🖨️ print button at the top of the freebies page — it produces a clean print-friendly view of all freebies including which ones you've claimed. Save as PDF from your browser's print menu.
How often is this updated?
Candice Klein reviews and updates freebies regularly. Each card shows a verified date. Found a change? Send it via WhatsApp on the Contact page!

💡 Tips & Guides

Get the most out of every birthday freebie!

📅 4 Weeks Before Your Birthday

1Sign up for all restaurant loyalty apps — Spur, Mugg & Bean, Nando's, Steers, Krispy Kreme, Kauai & RocoMamas. Most require prior membership to unlock the birthday freebie.

2Register your birthday on Dis-Chem, Clicks, The Body Shop, Sorbet & Truworths — vouchers are sent via email and you'll miss them if you sign up late.

3Book the Stellenbosch Wine Tractor and Durbanville Wine Safari well in advance — both are extremely popular.

4Sign up for the Cape Wheel Birthday Club at capewheel.co.za/clubs — your voucher is emailed on your birthday so register before.

5Join Ster-Kinekor SK Club and buy at least 1 ticket first to activate your free birthday ticket.

🎂 Ideal Birthday Day Plan

Table Mountain (morning) → Two Oceans Aquarium → Cape Wheel → Waterfront Duck Tour → dinner at RocoMamas or Nando's. That's 5 freebies in one day — all free with your SA ID!

Most restaurant freebies are valid 7–30 days around your birthday — spread them across the month rather than rushing everything into one day.

❓ FAQ

Everything you need to know about birthday freebies.
What if they refuse my freebie?
Politely ask for a manager, show your SA ID and loyalty app. Contact the brand's customer service if still refused. Always call ahead first — this app is a guide and offers can change.
Do I always need my SA ID?
Yes — almost always. A green ID book, smart ID card, driver's licence or passport is accepted. Always carry it!
Can I claim freebies on a different day?
Many yes — most restaurant freebies are valid 7 days before/after. Table Mountain and Two Oceans are valid the whole birthday month. Robben Island, Total Ninja and UpCycles require your actual birthday.
Are wine farm freebies really free?
Usually a complimentary glass or tasting — not a free full meal. Always call ahead to confirm what is included and whether a meal booking is required.
When should I sign up for loyalty apps?
At least 4 weeks before your birthday. Krispy Kreme requires 48hrs minimum; Sorbet and SK Club require prior activity to activate your birthday reward.
Can kids claim freebies too?
Yes! Use the 👶 Kids filter to see all child-friendly freebies. Always bring a birth certificate or ID as proof of age and birthday.
How do I save this as a phone app?
iPhone/iPad: Open in Safari → tap Share ⬆️ → Add to Home Screen. Android: Open in Chrome → tap ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen. Works offline once saved!
How often is this app updated?
Candice Klein reviews and updates freebies regularly. Each card shows a verified date. Found a change or a new freebie? Send it via WhatsApp on the Contact page!
